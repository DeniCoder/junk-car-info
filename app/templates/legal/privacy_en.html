PRIVACY POLICY

(GDPR Compliant)

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1. This Privacy Policy (hereinafter — the Policy) defines the procedure for processing and protecting personal data of users of the "Junked Vehicles Map" service (hereinafter — the Service).

1.2. The personal data operator is the Service administration (hereinafter — the Operator).

1.3. Use of the Service means unconditional consent of the user to this Policy.

2. PURPOSES OF DATA PROCESSING

2.1. Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

  • User registration and identification;
  • Providing access to Service functionality;
  • Content moderation;
  • Sending notifications about publication status;
  • Ensuring security and preventing fraud;
  • Compliance with legal requirements.

3. TYPES OF DATA COLLECTED

3.1. The Service processes the following personal data:

  • Username (pseudonym);
  • Email address;
  • Registration date and time;
  • IP address and device information;
  • Geolocation data of published findings;
  • Photos uploaded by the user.

3.2. The Service does NOT process:

  • Passport data;
  • Phone numbers;
  • Financial information;
  • Biometric data.

4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

4.1. Processing is based on:

  • Consent of the data subject (GDPR Art. 6, 9);
  • Contract for provision of services (GDPR Art. 6);
  • Legitimate interests of the Operator (GDPR Art. 6).

5. DATA RETENTION PERIOD

5.1. Personal data is stored until the user deletes their account or the Service ceases operations.

5.2. After account deletion, data is anonymized within 30 days.

6. DATA SECURITY MEASURES

6.1. The Operator implements necessary technical and organizational measures:

  • Password encryption (bcrypt/Werkzeug);
  • CSRF protection;
  • Regular backups;
  • Restricted database access;
  • User activity logging.

7. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR)

7.1. You have the right to:

  • Right of access: Obtain information about processing of your data;
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Right to restriction: Request blocking of processing;
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in machine-readable format;
  • Right to object: Object to processing of your data;
  • Right to lodge a complaint: File a complaint with supervisory authority.

8. HOW TO EXERCISE YOUR RIGHTS

8.1. To exercise your rights, you can:

  • Delete your account through profile settings;
  • Send a request to email: privacy@junkcar.tech

8.2. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

9. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

9.1. All servers are located in the Russian Federation.

9.2. For EU users: Data transfers outside the EU are protected by appropriate safeguards.

10. COOKIES

10.1. The Service uses cookies for:

  • Session management (necessary);
  • Language preferences (necessary);
  • Security (CSRF tokens).

10.2. You can manage cookie settings in your browser.
